# Point-in-time universe — construction, completeness, known gaps

Rule (backtest/pit_universe.py): on each decision session D, the universe is the hundred largest US-listed domestic filers (10-K/10-Q) by market cap at the close of D-1 = share count from the latest periodic filing accepted before sixteen hundred ET on D-1 (cover-page dei EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding if consistent with the same filing's weighted count, else the weighted count, else net income over diluted EPS) x split factor x split-adjusted close of D-1. Membership is computed inside the harness from snapshot data, so the poisoned-future tests cover it. <!-- text-only -->

- Seed two: every company listed today on NYSE/Nasdaq/CBOE (SEC company_tickers_exchange.json) with a hundred million or more shares in any EDGAR frame since `2013-01-01`. Pool: 2,478 [SOURCED FACT · backtest/universe/pit/candidates.json, 2026-11-04] companies.
- Screen: EDGAR frames share counts x yfinance month-end closes at every quarter end in `2014-03-31..2026-09-30`; candidate if ranked two hundred or better at any quarter end (screen values can be overstated, which only enlarges the pool).
- Seed three: listed companies ranked one hundred fifty or better by public float (dei EntityPublicFloat, a USD market value on every 10-K cover, needs no prices) in any year in `2014-01-01..2025-12-31`. This catches multi-class filers with no usable share count in the frames (Visa) and high-priced names below the share threshold (MercadoLibre). <!-- text-only -->

**Effect of the gaps.** Missing names cannot enter the ranking, so the hundredth slot goes to the next company (the universe is complete in size but slightly too small in average cap), and their events are absent. Most are acquisitions (Celgene, Allergan, Monsanto, Time Warner, Express Scripts, EMC, Anadarko, Shire), Walgreens (taken private) and Dow Chemical (DowDuPont merger). The direction of the bias is unknown: several were long decliners before they left (Walgreens, Allergan, Celgene, Express Scripts), and a takeover premium lands on one announcement day, rarely inside an earnings window (independent review P1-5). The reviewer estimates the missing events at a few percent of the 2015-2026 sample; the within-ticker H1 statistic is largely immune.
