# Delisted-company gap — Session 4 Phase A

No delisted-price provider is configured for this environment (no API key or network secret; allowed hosts: Yahoo, SEC, FRED, Hugging Face, Finnhub, Gemini). Yahoo keeps no history for delisted tickers and Finnhub candles return 403 on this plan. The gap therefore stays as documented, and is sized here from EDGAR only. <!-- text-only -->

Method (backtest/delisted_gap.py): gap companies are the public-float top 150 of any year with no current listing and a plausible float; their earnings events are 8-K Item 2.02 filings reduced to one per reporting period; an event counts as lost if the latest public float known before D (at most 460 days old) is at least the 100th-largest point-in-time market cap of the candidate pool on the float's date. The float understates market cap, so this is a lower bound; using the 150th-largest gives an upper bound. Window: D in 2015-01-01..2026-09-24 (the H1 sample window). <!-- text-only -->
