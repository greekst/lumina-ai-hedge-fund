# Delisted-company gap — Session 4 Phase A

No delisted-price provider is configured for this environment (no API key or network secret; allowed hosts: Yahoo, SEC, FRED, Hugging Face, Finnhub, Gemini). Yahoo keeps no history for delisted tickers and Finnhub candles return 403 on this plan. The gap therefore stays as documented, and is sized here from EDGAR only. <!-- text-only -->

Method (backtest/delisted_gap.py, revised after the Session 4 independent review, P1-1): gap companies are the top one hundred fifty by public float among plausible filers (float at most fifteen times the company's largest total assets; ranked after that filter) in any year, with no current common-stock listing (a company whose only listing is a preferred share, such as E. I. du Pont, counts), dropping a float value that is more than twice both neighbouring years' values (Discover's mis-tagged 2022 float); their earnings events are 8-K Item 2.02 filings reduced to one per reporting period; an event counts as lost if the latest public float known before D (at most 460 days old) is at least the 100th-largest point-in-time market cap of the candidate pool on the float's date. The float understates market cap, so this is a lower bound; using the 150th-largest gives a PARTIAL upper bound: it covers only the companies this float screen can find. Window: D in 2015-01-01..2026-09-30 (the H1 sample window). <!-- text-only -->
